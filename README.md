# Fruit Catcher 2D

A fast-paced, arcade-style 2D casual game built with Unity. Control your basket, catch as many falling fruits as possible before the timer runs out, and aim for the high score!

---

## Gameplay Overview

In **Fruit Catcher 2D**, delicious fruits rain down from the top of the screen at random intervals and positions. Your goal is simple: slide your plate left and right across the bottom of the screen to catch falling fruits. 

- **Time-Attack Format:** Each run features a ticking countdown timer (default: **60 seconds**).
- **Fruit Values:** Different fruits award different point tiers.
- **Hazards & Penalties:** Avoid rotten fruits or obstacles that deduct points or reduce your remaining time.
- **Skill:** PLayer recharge skill through fruits catched and activating the skills increase player movement.
